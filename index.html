I'm a senior Flutter engineer who also designs. I lead mobile at 3Scorers, I've shipped banking and savings apps used across Nigeria, and I designed and built my own game, Brick Fury, from the first sketch to the store listing.

That mix is the point. I can take an idea from a Figma frame to a Rive animation to tested, production code, and I care as much about why a screen works as how it's built. On the backend I work in Go and Node, so the whole product stays in one pair of hands when it needs to.
